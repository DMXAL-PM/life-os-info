Life OS Personal

Life OS Personal brings projects, tasks, ideas and a connected Google Calendar into one personal workspace. The application and its database run on the owner’s Mac.

This website provides application and privacy information. It does not host the Life OS application, offer public account registration, or collect calendar information.

Google Calendar, inside your workspace

The owner connects a Google account, selects calendars, and views a saved agenda alongside tasks. Life OS reads calendar events to show availability. With edit access, it can create or change an event after the owner reviews and approves the exact change.

Optional AI planning

The owner can review the exact planning input and approve a request through their OpenAI subscription. Suggestions are saved for review. Sending a calendar change requires separate approval inside Life OS.

Your data and choices

Read the privacy policy for the data accessed, local storage, optional sharing and disconnection controls. Usage terms explain the personal-use scope.

Contact
